Scanning

Scan on your phone.
Filed on your computer.

  1. Tap the owl. The camera finds the page's edges; add as many pages as you like.
  2. It lands in your inbox, one PDF in iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive.
  3. Rupert reads it on your computer and suggests a title, date, tags and folder.
  4. One click files it, renamed, with its details written into the PDF.

Everything else a good librarian does

Add the folders you already keep documents in. Rupert shows them as they are and keeps up with every change, whether you make it in Rupert, the Finder or File Explorer.
