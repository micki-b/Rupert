Rupert, the librarian for your documents

Search the text of every scanned document, let new scans name and file themselves, and ask Rupert where things are. Rupert works on the folders you already have, so there's nothing to import and nothing to lock you in.

Download, free iPhone app

What Rupert does

Your folders stay your library

Add the folders you already keep documents in: on your computer, in iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive. Rupert shows them as they are and keeps up with changes.

Find anything

Search titles, tags, notes and the text inside every PDF, with the matching words highlighted. Browse as a list, as large icons, or in a gallery that flips through the pages.

An inbox that files itself

New scans get their text recognized on your computer, and Rupert suggests a title, date, tags and folder. Check it and file it with a click.

Details that travel with the file

Titles, tags, dates and notes are stored inside the PDF (or in a small hidden file next to other kinds), so they go wherever the file goes.

Ask Rupert

Ask in plain words, like "How much did the plumber charge in April?". Rupert searches your library and answers from your own documents, with a model on your computer by default.

Private by design

No account with us, no tracking, no ads. Text recognition and filing suggestions stay on your computer. Online-only files aren't downloaded until you open one.

On your iPhone

Browse and search the same library on your iPhone, open any document, and star the folders you use most. Scan paper with the camera straight into your inbox, where Rupert on your computer reads it and suggests where it belongs.

Works with folders in iCloud Drive and on your iPhone, OneDrive (with your Microsoft account) and, as a preview, Google Drive (with your Google account).
