Support

Help inside the app

On a computer, Rupert's help covers every feature: Help ▸ Rupert Help, or press F1. It explains adding folders, the inbox, scanning, online-only files, Ask Rupert and setting up AI models, and the iPhone app.

Report a problem

Please open a bug report on GitHub. The form asks for what's needed to look into it:

  • Rupert's version: on a computer it's shown at the bottom left of the window; on iPhone in Rupert's Settings ▸ About.
  • Your system and its version (for example macOS 15, Windows 11 or iOS 18), and your device.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps that lead to it.

Please leave out private document contents, account details and API keys from screenshots and logs.

Ideas and requests

Suggestions are welcome as a feature request on GitHub. You can also see what's being worked on.

Privacy

Rupert collects nothing about you. The privacy policy explains what happens to your information.
